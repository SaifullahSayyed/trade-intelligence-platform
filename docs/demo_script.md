## Opening (2 min)

"What you are about to see is the working platform infrastructure for a trade intelligence product built on U.S. CBP ocean manifest data.

Every screen you will see is a **live, running system** — not slides, not mockups.

Before I share my screen I need to give you four pieces of context that are legally and factually important:

1. The data you will see is **synthetic sample data** — it mirrors the structure of the Trademo evaluation dataset exactly, but uses hand-constructed name variants to validate our entity-matching logic. Real Trademo data ingestion is pending AWS Data Exchange subscription approval.

2. The entity resolution metrics — 10 matched pairs out of 18 candidates — come from a real run of our matching pipeline against those synthetic variants. These numbers are **preliminary and directional**. They show the logic works mechanically. They do not constitute a statistically robust benchmark.

3. There has been **no counsel-reviewed legal sign-off** on compliance claims as of today.

4. We have **no paying customers yet**.

With that said — let me show you what this platform actually does."

## Section 2 — Manifest Search (3 min)

*[Navigate to /search.]*

"This is the manifest search interface. You can query by company name, HS code, Bill of Lading number, or goods description.

Notice the header badge: 'Synthetic Evaluation Sample — Manifest Search Prototype.' Again, that is not us being modest — that is the system being honest about its data state.

The search returns all shipment records associated with Walmart — across every variant of the name that appears in customs filings: 'Walmart Inc.,' 'WAL-MART STORES, INC.,' 'WALMART STORES EAST LP.' Our entity resolution layer has clustered these into a single canonical entity. That is the core value proposition.

Entity coverage shown here: '10 of 18 pairs matched.' Real data will replace this once ingestion is live."

## Section 3 — Company Profile & AI Explanation (5 min)

*[Click into a Walmart record, then the AI Grounded Explanation tab.]*

"Each canonical entity has a Company Profile. The metrics you see — Total Sample Volume, Candidate Coverage 10/18 — are calculated from the synthetic benchmark run.

The AI Explanation panel is important to understand. This is not a chatbot. Our AI model is constrained by what we call a 'Banned Patterns Filter.' It cannot say a company is 'compliant,' 'clean,' or 'low risk.' It can only summarize verified facts from the underlying shipment records, with citations.

*[Read the safety disclaimer aloud:]*

'Synthetic test data used to validate entity-matching logic only — not derived from any real shipment records.'

When real data is ingested, the AI will summarize from real CBP manifest fields. The safety constraints remain the same."

---

## Section 4 — Human Review Queue (3 min)

*[Navigate to /review.]*

"Any pair our algorithm is uncertain about — score between 0.65 and 0.85 — goes here for a human decision. Today there are 8 pairs in the queue.

This is Brief §6 in our spec: the false-merge rate guardrail. Merging two distinct companies is a worse error than flagging a true match for review. The queue ensures a human always has the final word on ambiguous cases.

Every decision a reviewer makes is written to an immutable audit table in PostgreSQL. You can see that on the right: 'Logged decisions written to PostgreSQL entity_resolution_audit table.'"

The status pill reads 'LOCAL DEV STACK ONLINE | POSTGRES · CLICKHOUSE · MINIO.' In production this would read the live environment status."

## FAQ Prep

**Q: Why not use real data today?**
A: We are pending AWS Data Exchange subscription approval from Trademo. The subscription has been applied for. This is a vendor process timeline, not a technical blocker.

**Q: Are the entity resolution numbers real?**
A: They are real pipeline outputs — the code actually ran and produced these numbers. The inputs are synthetic name variants designed to test the matching logic, not a sample of real shipment records. The methodology is real; the input data is synthetic.

**Q: How long until you have real data?**
A: The moment the subscription is approved, our hot-swap checklist is one working day.

**Q: What does "no legal sign-off" mean?**
A: No lawyer has reviewed specific compliance claim language yet. The platform does not make compliance claims — but we want to be transparent that the framing has not been reviewed by counsel.
